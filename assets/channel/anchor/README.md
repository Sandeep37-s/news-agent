Preserve the master face, complexion, hairline, side-parted wavy black hair, dark eyes, clean-shaven jaw and adult proportions. Preserve the navy blazer, ivory open-collar shirt and charcoal trousers. Keep his natural Indian appearance consistent; do not lighten his skin, substitute a different face or add stereotyped costume details. Use the supplied angle, expression and pose references for the same character. The master wins if a supporting panel drifts. Expressions and gestures should suit the story; namaste is optional for a greeting, never required in every scene. He is a fictional illustrated presenter, not a real journalist or evidence of a real event.

These are 2D raster reference sheets with ivory backgrounds, not photographs, transparent cutouts, vector artwork, mouth-shape assets or an animation rig. Prepare and review separate animation layers as needed. The speaking expression is one expression, not a complete lip-sync mouth set. Inspect hand anatomy and identity continuity before final animation. Do not claim animation assets exist until they have been prepared.
