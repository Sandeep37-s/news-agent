# Permanent Channel Bible

Channel language: Hindi. Primary focus: India, including Bollywood and regional Indian cinema.
Major global developments are information alerts until the human explicitly selects a story.
Aim for under five minutes where accuracy and clarity allow. No filler.
Style: cinematic documentary explainer with consistent 2D anime-inspired original illustrations.
Use an original fictional male illustrated anchor, male Hindi narration, and the approved permanent anchor reference and newsroom design from the Claude project's channel files.
Do not imply the fictional anchor is a real journalist or public figure.
Keep source links and claim-to-source references in research notes and the proposed video description. Preserve uncertainty and the distinction between claims and established facts.
Narration should flow naturally. Do not routinely say "according to BBC", "this website reports", or read publisher names and URLs aloud in every video or after every fact.
Mention a source in narration only when its identity is essential to understanding or evaluating the claim: a person's tweet/post or public statement, a direct quotation, an allegation, an exclusive or uncorroborated report, disputed information, or a materially uncertain estimate. Identify the actual speaker/account/authority where known and relevant, rather than mechanically naming an outlet that repeated it.
For a tweet or public post, make clear who posted it and what they stated; do not present the post itself as proof that the underlying claim is true. If the original post has not been verified, say so when material.
Routine independently established background facts need no repetitive spoken source mention. Use concise on-screen attribution only where needed for context, uncertainty, licensing, or a graphic; retain the full evidence trail outside the narration. Never remove essential attribution in a way that makes a claim misleading or falsely suggests original reporting.
Never portray generated illustrations as authentic news photos or footage.
Use original or properly licensed assets. Film footage, posters, music, and photographs require appropriate permission or another valid basis for use.
Human approval is required at research, Hindi script, storyboard/visual plan, and final video review stages.
Publishing always requires separate explicit authorization.

## Original-story workflow
Treat publisher feeds as private discovery leads. Select only stories with meaningful public interest and sufficient evidence for further production research. Research important claims using original statements, documents and independent reporting before treating them as established facts.
Develop our own story structure, Hindi narration, explanation and analysis. Do not copy a publisher's script or rewrite it sentence by sentence. News links do not grant permission to reuse articles, footage, film clips, photos or music; use original illustrations and properly licensed assets.
For headline-only records, state in the production research review that article text was not collected and further research is required. Do not turn a headline or repeated coverage into a confirmed fact.
The human selects the story and approves production stages. Publishing remains a separate explicitly authorized action.
